# WealthPulse — Deploy Guide
# ================================
# Complete this in under 20 minutes.

## STEP 1: Get a free Netlify account
1. Go to https://netlify.com
2. Click "Sign up" — use your email or GitHub
3. It's completely free

## STEP 2: Deploy your site (drag and drop)
1. In Netlify dashboard, find the box that says "Drag and drop your site folder here"
2. Drag the entire "wealthpulse-blog" folder onto it
3. Netlify gives you a live URL instantly (e.g. random-name.netlify.app)
4. Your site is LIVE

## STEP 3: Get a custom domain (optional, ~£10/year)
1. Go to https://porkbun.com or https://namecheap.com
2. Search for your domain (e.g. wealthpulse.co.uk)
3. Buy it (~£8–12/year)
4. In Netlify: Site Settings → Domain Management → Add Custom Domain
5. Follow the DNS instructions (takes ~10 minutes to propagate)

## STEP 4: Google AdSense (the money part)
1. Go to https://adsense.google.com
2. Sign up with your Google account
3. Enter your site URL
4. Add the verification code to your pages (instructions provided by Google)
5. Wait for approval (2–4 weeks typically)
6. Once approved, replace every "Ad Placeholder" comment in the HTML files
   with your actual AdSense <ins> code blocks

## STEP 5: Affiliate Links (more money)
Sign up FREE at:
- Amazon Associates: https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk
- ClickBank: https://clickbank.com (approved same day)
- ShareASale: https://shareasale.com
- Impact: https://impact.com

Once you have your links, paste them into the SleepStack engine
and it will inject them automatically into every new article.

## STEP 6: Keep growing with SleepStack
1. Run SleepStack to generate new articles
2. Click "Copy HTML" on each article
3. Create a new .html file in the articles/ folder
4. Paste the HTML
5. Add the page to sitemap.xml
6. Re-upload the folder to Netlify (drag and drop again)

## REVENUE EXPECTATIONS (realistic)
- Month 1-3:  Traffic building. £0–£5/month from ads.
- Month 4-6:  Google starts ranking content. £10–£50/month.
- Month 6-12: Compounding. £50–£300/month.
- Year 2+:    £300–£1,500+/month with consistent publishing.

The key: keep adding articles. Every article is a permanent asset.
The more content, the more traffic, the more passive income.
